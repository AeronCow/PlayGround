    How to Play

    ❖

    Touch

    Left thumb moves. Drag the right side to look. Hold the pickaxe to mine or strike. The cube places the selected block. Jump and interact buttons appear where needed.

    Desktop

    WASD move · Mouse look · Left click mine · Right click place · Space jump · E interact · 1–6 blocks · Tab journal.

    Tilt

    Open the menu and enable Tilt to Look. On iPhone and iPad, iOS will ask for motion access — allow it, then tilt the device gently. Recenter by toggling it off and on.

    Adventure

    Follow the gold compass markers. Gather wood and stone, recover the three Crown Shards, and return them to Elder Rowan. Beware the brigands and the Black Knight.
